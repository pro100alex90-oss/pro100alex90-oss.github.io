PicShift

Convert HEIC photos to JPG, PNG or WebP, shrink them and remove the location — on your Mac.

How to use

  1. Drag photos or folders into the PicShift window (or onto its Dock icon).
  2. Choose the format, size and privacy options on the right.
  3. Click Convert. Results appear in Downloads › PicShift unless you chose another folder.

FAQ

PicShift asks to allow a folder. When you save “Next to originals”, macOS asks you once to allow PicShift to write into that folder. PicShift remembers your choice.

The free version stops at 10 photos. Convert them in groups of 10, or get PicShift Pro — a one-time purchase.

Restore PicShift Pro on another Mac. PicShift Pro → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.
