Mahjong Salon

Mahjong solitaire for Mac: match pairs of free tiles until the board is clear.

How to play

  • A tile is free when nothing lies on top of it and its left or right side is open.
  • Click two free tiles with the same picture to remove them. Any flower matches any flower, any season matches any season.
  • Every deal can be cleared. If you run out of pairs, use Shuffle (⌥⌘S) or Undo (⌘Z). ⇧⌘H shows a hint.

FAQ

“These tiles can't all be cleared.” Sometimes the last tiles end up stacked so that no arrangement can be finished. Undo a few moves and try another path.

Restore Mahjong Salon Pro on another Mac. Open Mahjong Salon Pro from the app menu and choose Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.
