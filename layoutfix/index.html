LayoutFix

Fixes text typed in the wrong keyboard layout — “ghbdtn” becomes “привет”, “руддщ” becomes “hello”.

Getting started

  1. Open LayoutFix. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
  2. Allow Input Monitoring and Accessibility when asked (System Settings → Privacy & Security).
  3. Relaunch LayoutFix if asked. Just type — words in the wrong layout are fixed after Space.

Shortcuts (default)

  • ⌥⇧Space — fix the last word / undo a fix / cycle layouts
  • ⌥⇧K — convert selected text
  • ⌥⇧U — change case of selection
  • ⌥⇧T — transliterate selection
  • ⌥⇧A — turn auto-correct on/off

FAQ

A word was fixed by mistake. Press ⌥⇧Space right away: the fix is undone and the word is remembered as an exception.

It doesn't work in one app. Check that the app isn't in Settings → Exceptions. In some apps, increase “Delay between typed characters”.
