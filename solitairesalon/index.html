Solitaire Salon

Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, Pyramid and TriPeaks for Mac.

How to play

  • Drag a card or a run of cards onto another pile. Double-click a card to send it where it fits.
  • Click the deck to deal. In Pyramid, click two cards that add up to 13 (a King goes alone). In TriPeaks, click a card one higher or lower than the open card.
  • ⌘Z undoes a move, ⇧⌘H shows a hint, ⌘N starts a new game, ⌘D opens the Daily Challenge.

FAQ

Restore Solitaire Salon Pro on another Mac. Open Solitaire Salon Pro from the app menu and choose Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Replay a FreeCell deal. Every game shows its number; FreeCell numbers match the classic numbered deals.
