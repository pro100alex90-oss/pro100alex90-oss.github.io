TableTidy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. TableTidy does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking, and the app has no network access.

Your files

TableTidy reads only the files you open and writes only where you choose to save. Your files are processed on your Mac and never leave it. Settings and the list of recent files are stored only on your Mac.

Purchases

TableTidy Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
